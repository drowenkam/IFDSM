F I N A L S E C T I O N

Mock examination — 60 questions

Sixty single-best-answer items sampling all ten competencies in proportion. Work through the whole paper in one sitting without notes, allowing about 60 minutes. Mark yourself against the answer key that follows, which gives the parent topic for every item so you can go back to the source rather than merely learn the answer. A score of 48 or above (80%) suggests you are on track for a strong pass; anything below 42 means returning to the topics where you dropped marks before attempting the paper again.
