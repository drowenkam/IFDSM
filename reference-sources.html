About this notebook. The content, structure and sub-topic numbering follow the candidate's own ten-topic syllabus notes and lecture material. All clinical content has been cross-checked against, and where necessary corrected and expanded from, the current primary

sources listed above — in particular the 2015 AASM/AADSM clinical practice guideline, the 2025 AADSM standards of practice, the 2023 AASM Scoring Manual version 3, and ICSD-3-TR. Where the source notes reflected superseded criteria — the four-stage NREM nomenclature, the one-month insomnia duration criterion, the 1990 fibromyalgia tender-point criteria, and the ≥4% hypopnoea desaturation rule — both the classic and the current position are given, with the discrepancy flagged in an exam-trap box. Figures are reproduced from the candidate's own source documents. Reimbursement and referral pathways are jurisdiction-specific and are flagged as such throughout; verify local rules against the current official schedule before relying on them clinically.
