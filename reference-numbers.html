C. Oral appliance therapy
ParameterValue Note
Initial protrusive setting50–70% of maximum protru-sion (AADSM 2025: 25–75% acceptable) Do not start at maximum; >75% predisposes to TMD
Vertical opening at construction bite~2 mm interincisal (grey George Gauge fork) Increasing vertical opening reduces efficacy and promotes mouth breathing
Minimum titration increment≤1 mm (commonly 0.1–0.4 mm per turn) Definition of an effective appliance requires ≥5 mm adjustment range and reversibility
Minimum protrusive capacity for a MAD≥5–6 mm (normal 10–12 mm) <6 mm is a relative contraindication
Minimum dentition6–10 sound teeth per arch with adequate bone support Sources vary; state the principle (adequate retention and anchorage)
Titration period~2–5 months (longer than CPAP) Reassess within 30 days of insertion

Bariatric surgery thresholdBMI ≥35 with comorbidity, after failed non-surgical weight loss Reduces AHI substantially but frequently does not cure OSA
